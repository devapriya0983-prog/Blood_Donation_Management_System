About LifeSaver

Who We Are

LifeSaver is a Blood Donation Management System designed to connect blood donors with people who need blood.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make blood donation easier, faster and more accessible by connecting donors and recipients through a simple online platform.

What We Do

Users can register as donors, search for available donors, submit blood requests and track their request status.

Our Vision

We aim to build a supportive community where technology helps people connect during blood donation needs.
